# 2-Minute Recruiter Demo

> **Live Demo:** [https://ai-business-intelligence-studio.onrender.com](https://ai-business-intelligence-studio.onrender.com)

Follow this structured 2-minute walkthrough to evaluate the complete end-to-end capabilities of the **AI Business Intelligence Studio**.

---

## ⏱️ Step-by-Step Demo Guide

### 1. Open the Live Demo
Open the live demo URL above in any modern browser (desktop recommended).

### 2. Load a Sample Dataset (1-Click Exploration)
On the landing page, select any of the preloaded sample datasets:
- **🛍️ Retail Sales**: Regional sales trends, product margins, and seasonal spikes.
- **👥 HR Analytics**: Departmental turnover, salary patterns, and tenure analysis.
- **📊 Customer Churn**: Contract duration retention and support ticket frequency.

*(No manual CSV download or file upload is required).*

### 3. Inspect Data Quality & Profiles
Scroll to **Data Overview**:
- View the automated **Data Quality Score (0–100)** calculated from completeness, uniqueness, and consistency.
- Click on any column pill (e.g., `Salary` or `Revenue`) to open the statistical inspection drawer displaying `Mean`, `Median`, `Std Dev`, `Min/Max`, and `Skewness`.

### 4. Review Top Findings & Business Insights
Examine the **Top 3 Findings** and **Key Business Insights**:
- Note the prioritized findings generated from statistical discovery algorithms.
- Read the plain-English explanation and italicized **Strategic Takeaway** for each finding.

### 5. Explore Visual Evidence
Scroll to **Supporting Evidence**:
- Inspect the interactive Plotly visualizations (time-series trends, distribution histograms, category comparisons, and correlation matrix heatmaps).
- Hover over data points to view detailed values.

### 6. Run Prediction Studio (AutoML Lite)
Scroll to **Prediction Studio**:
- Select a target business metric from the dropdown (e.g., `Revenue` or `Attrition`).
- Click **Analyze Drivers**.

### 7. Review Important Drivers & Model Confidence
- Examine the cross-validated confidence metric ($R^2$ Score or Accuracy).
- Review the plain-English driver summary and the **Key Drivers** horizontal bar chart showing normalized feature importances.

### 8. Generate & Download Reports
Scroll to **Export Report**:
- Click **View Full Report** to open a clean, styled HTML report in a new tab.
- Click **Download PDF** to generate and download a multi-page PDF report with embedded high-resolution chart graphics.
